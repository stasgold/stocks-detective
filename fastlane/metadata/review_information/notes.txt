Stocks Detective explains why the stocks in a portfolio moved. No account or sign-in is needed.

Quick start for review: on first launch tap "Try the demo portfolio". This loads five sample holdings with built-in sample prices and headlines (Settings > Demo data), so every screen works offline and every verdict type appears.

- Tap a holding to open its case file (verdict, causes, evidence, chart).
- "Interrogate the detective" opens a follow-up chat. It needs Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later with Apple Intelligence on); other devices get the built-in rule-based analyst and a message explaining this.
- The Briefing tab summarizes the whole portfolio.
- Settings > "Alert me about unusual moves" asks for notification permission; alerts are sent only from background refresh.

All AI runs on-device with Apple's Foundation Models; the portfolio never leaves the device. Live prices and headlines come from public Yahoo Finance and Google News feeds and may be delayed.

The app is educational and gives no investment advice: it never tells users to buy or sell, and it shows a disclaimer on every analysis screen. It does not trade, hold funds, or connect to brokerages.
