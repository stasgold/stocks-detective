Most apps tell you “AAPL is down 3%.” Stocks Detective tells you why.

Every holding becomes a case file:
• What happened – the move in plain language, with the numbers that matter.
• Which news caused it – the headlines that best explain the move, with sources.
• Is it justified? – a verdict: Justified, Overreaction, Underreaction, Unexplained or Just noise.
• What to watch next – specific things to check in the coming days.

HOW IT WORKS
The detective compares each stock with its sector ETF and the S&P 500 to separate market moves from company news, measures how unusual the move is against the stock's normal day and volume, and ranks the headlines by relevance.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Reports are written on-device by Apple Intelligence, so your portfolio never leaves your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, a built-in rule-based analyst explains every move.

FEATURES
• Daily Briefing: one story for your whole portfolio and who moved it
• Interrogate the detective: ask follow-up questions about any case
• Background alerts only for unusual, stock-specific moves
• Import holdings by pasting a table from your broker or a spreadsheet
• Interactive price charts from 1 day to all time
• Demo portfolio to try it instantly

Stocks Detective explains market moves for educational purposes. It is not investment advice. Market data may be delayed.
