# Stocks Detective Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Stocks Detective explains why the stocks in your portfolio moved. It is built so that your portfolio stays on your device.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
- The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data.

## Data that stays on your device

- **Your holdings** (tickers and quantities) are stored only on your iPhone.
- **Settings** (for example, whether alerts are on) are stored in the app's local preferences.
- **AI analysis** runs on-device with Apple Intelligence (Apple's Foundation Models). Your portfolio and questions are not sent to any AI service.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

## Network requests

To show prices and news, the app requests public market data directly from:

- Yahoo Finance (prices, charts and ticker search)
- Google News (headlines)

These requests contain the ticker symbols or search text needed for the lookup, plus the standard information any internet request carries, such as your IP address. They contain no account, name or identifier from the app. The services' own privacy policies apply to those requests:

- Yahoo: https://legal.yahoo.com/us/en/yahoo/privacy/index.html
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Notifications and background refresh

If you turn on "Alert me about unusual moves", the app uses iOS background refresh to check your holdings and shows local notifications. These notifications are generated on the device. No push notification service is used.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
